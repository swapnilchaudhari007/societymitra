# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want **SocietyMitra** to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and constructive
- Give feedback on the work, not the person
- Assume good intent and ask before judging
- Credit others' ideas and code
- Help newcomers make their first contribution

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or jokes
- Publishing others' private information
- Spamming issues or PRs, including bulk low-effort AI-generated content
- Any conduct inappropriate in a professional setting

## AI-assisted work

You own what you submit. Review and test AI-generated changes, say when AI helped, and keep PRs focused.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer, [@swapnilchaudhari007](https://github.com/swapnilchaudhari007). Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Responses range from a private note to a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on severity.
